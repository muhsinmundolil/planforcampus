Campus Architecture Features
  • Enclosed Girls Enclave: Integrated G+4 Academic Complex (+1/+2 and Day School, 16 classrooms $\times$ 60 = 960 students) directly connected to the G+3 internal courtyard hostel.
  • Centroid Sports Hub: 25m semi-Olympic pool, double-court sports hall, 200m athletic track, and soccer field accessible centrally to all students.
  • Triple Segregated Access: Gate 1 (North) for KG & parents, Gate 2 (West) for General CBSE, Gate 3 (South) dedicated biometric security gate for Girls Enclave.
  • IBC Code & Safety: Dual 2.0m scissor fire stairwells in every wing, 56 WCs in girls complex, and 70 WCs / 85 showers in hostel.
