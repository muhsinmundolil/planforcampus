## 2. Girls Residential Hostel (G+4 Floors — Courtyard Model)
* **Structural Footprint:** $38.0\text{ m} \times 26.0\text{ m}$ surrounding a $22.0\text{ m} \times 10.0\text{ m}$ open lightwell courtyard.
* **Total BUA:** $5,000\text{ m}^2$ ($55,000\text{ sq ft}$).
* **Total Residential Beds:** 650–700 Boarders.
* **Corridors:** $2.4\text{ m}$ ($8'\text{-}0''$) wide perimeter balcony corridors opening into the courtyard.

### Floors 1 through 4: Typical Dormitory Levels (160 Boarders per Floor)
* **Total Rooms per Floor:** 40 Quad-Sharing Dormitory Rooms.
* **Room Specifications ($5.5\text{ m} \times 4.2\text{ m}$ = $18' \times 14'$, 250 sq ft):**
  * 4 single beds ($6'\text{-}3'' \times 3'\text{-}0''$) with under-bed luggage storage.
  * 4 full-height laminate wardrobes with locks.
  * 4 private study carrels with task lighting and power outlets.
  * 1 wide balcony window overlooking the inner quadrangle or campus landscape.
* **Floor Amenities (Per Level):**
  * **Central Study Hall ($10.0\text{ m} \times 6.0\text{ m}$, 650 sq ft):** 40 quiet study stations with high-speed Wi-Fi.
  * **Pantry Station ($4.0\text{ m} \times 3.0\text{ m}$):** Water purification plant, electric hot-water dispensers, microwaves.
  * **Resident Deputy Warden Room ($4.2\text{ m} \times 4.0\text{ m}$):** Attached washroom for overnight teacher supervisor.
* **Sanitary Cores (2 per floor — East and West Wings):**
  * East Wing: 5 Showers, 5 WCs, 6 Vanity Basins, laundry pre-wash sinks.
  * West Wing: 5 Showers, 5 WCs, 6 Vanity Basins, sanitary disposal.
  * Continuous pressurized hot water backed by rooftop solar collectors.
