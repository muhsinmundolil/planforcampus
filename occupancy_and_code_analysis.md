#### Egress & Plumbing Requirements (Girls Academic)
* **Minimum Required Stair Width:** 490 occupants / upper floor $\times 0.3\text{ in} = 147\text{ inches}$ ($3.74\text{ m}$). Provided: Two $2.0\text{ m}$ ($78.7\text{ in}$) fire staircases at opposite wing ends ($4.0\text{ m}$ total $\to$ **Compliant**).
* **Plumbing Fixtures (Female Only Facility):**
  * IBC Table 2902.1 for Group E: 1 Water Closet (WC) per 30 female occupants.
  * Required: $1,680 \div 30 = \mathbf{56\text{ WCs}}$ distributed across 4 levels ($\approx 14\text{ WCs per floor}$).
  * Lavatories: 1 per 50 occupants ($\approx 34\text{ washbasins}$).
  * Sanitary napkin dispensers and smokeless incinerators mandatory in each core.

## 3. Site-Wide Life Safety & Emergency Access Plan

1. **Emergency Fire Tender Access Ring:**
   * A $6.0\text{ m}$ continuous hardstanding perimeter road runs around all 4 sides of the campus.
   * Turning radius at all 4 corners exceeds $12.0\text{ m}$ to accommodate fire engines.
2. **Fire Hydrant Network:**
   * Ring-main pressurized fire hydrant line with hydrants spaced every $45\text{ m}$ along the perimeter road.
   * Central underground fire water storage reservoir: **200,000 Liters** located beneath the Central Administration plaza.
3. **Girls Enclave Egress in Emergencies:**
   * In normal daily operations, the Girls Enclave is secured with perimeter walls and one guarded gate.
   * **Life Safety Override:** The dividing perimeter wall is equipped with two **electromagnetic panic-bar emergency exit gates** wired to the central fire alarm system. Upon alarm trigger, the gates instantly release fail-safe to allow evacuation directly onto the central 200m sports field.
