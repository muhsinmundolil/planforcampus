# 4.5-Acre Institutional School Campus Master Plan & Architectural Dossier

A comprehensive, code-compliant architectural master plan and interactive 3D WebGL digital twin for a **4.5-Acre (196,020 sq.ft)** institutional school campus.

---

## 🏛️ Campus Overview & Core Program

1. **Girls Senior Academic & Residential Enclave:**
   - **G+4 Academic Complex:** Dedicated to Girls Senior Secondary (+1 & +2) and Girls Day School (Grades 8–12).
   - **Capacity:** 16 classrooms × 60 students = **960 students** (+1 & +2) plus 1,440 students (Day School).
   - **G+3 Courtyard Residential Hostel:** Secure internal courtyard geometry providing natural stack-effect ventilation and direct covered breezeway connection to the academic wing.
   - **Perimeter & Security:** Fully enclosed perimeter boundary with dedicated **Gate 3 (South)** biometric security checkpoint.

2. **General CBSE School (Grades 1–10):**
   - **G+3 Academic Facility:** Co-ed school with dedicated **Gate 2 (West)** vehicular loop, drop-off, and administrative entry.

3. **Dedicated Kindergarten (KG) Complex:**
   - **G+1 Pod:** Single-story ground pod with child-safe soft play landscape and dedicated **Gate 1 (North)** parent drop-off zone.

4. **Campus Centroid Sports & Wellness Hub:**
   - **Centrally Located Amenities:** Accessible equally to all campus zones with timed access controls.
   - **Facilities:** 25m semi-Olympic competition swimming pool, double-court multi-sport indoor gymnasium (basketball, badminton, volleyball), 200m 6-lane athletic running track, natural grass soccer turf, and dual tennis/pickleball courts.

## 💻 Interactive Deliverables

- **3D Master Plan Digital Twin (`campus_masterplan_central_common_3d.html`):**
  - Interactive Three.js WebGL model with LOD 350+ physical brise-soleil facades and architectural massing.
  - Solar study time-of-day shadow simulation slider.
  - First-person pedestrian walk mode.
  - 10-scene cinematic drone tour with built-in real-time video recorder.
- **Architectural Floor Plans (`campus_floor_plans.html`):**
  - Interactive 2D CAD/SVG blueprint viewer across all floor levels (Ground to Level 4) with room labels, student capacities, and dimensions.
- **ALPA Material Palette (`campus_material_palette.html`):**
  - High-performance terracotta baguettes, exposed textured concrete, acoustic wood slats, and low-E double glazing.
- **Code & Life Safety Dossier (`occupancy_and_code_analysis.md`):**
  - IBC Table 1004.5 calculations, 4,475 peak occupant load, dual 2.0m scissor stair egress paths, and full plumbing fixture schedules.
